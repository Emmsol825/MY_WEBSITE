4d_farm

Founded in 2026: a platform where farmers can invest in advanced, technology-driven farming methods.

Overview

4d_farm is a web platform I founded in 2026. It lets farmers invest in advanced, technology-driven farming methods, bringing modern techniques within reach of more growers.

The idea

  1. The problem. Many farmers cannot access or afford modern farming technology on their own.
  2. The platform. A single place to discover and invest in technology-driven farming methods.
  3. The goal. Higher yields and better livelihoods through technology, an example of applying engineering thinking outside medicine.
