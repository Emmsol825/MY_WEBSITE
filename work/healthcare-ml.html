Machine Learning for Healthcare

Exploring how machine learning on biomedical signals and images can support diagnosis and prediction.

Overview

As a Biomedical Engineering student at MUHAS, I am interested in how classical machine learning and deep learning can work with biomedical signal and image data to support diagnostic and predictive tools.

Interests

  1. Healthcare innovation with AI. Tools that help clinicians make faster, better-informed decisions.
  2. Biomedical engineering research. Linking what I learn about medical devices with data-driven methods.
  3. AI beyond medicine. Applying the same methods in non-medical fields such as agriculture.

Hands-on background

My industrial practical training with medical devices at Bugando Medical Centre (2025) showed me where the data comes from and how equipment is used in real clinical settings.
