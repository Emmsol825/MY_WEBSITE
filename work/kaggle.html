Kaggle Competitions

Competing on Kaggle to practise data cleaning, modelling and evaluation on real datasets.

Overview

Kaggle competitions give real datasets, a clear target and a public leaderboard. I take part to sharpen practical skills: understanding a new dataset quickly, building a baseline, and improving it step by step.

What I practise

  1. Exploration. Reading the data, spotting gaps and outliers, and forming hypotheses.
  2. Modelling. Building baselines, then trying stronger models and ensembles.
  3. Validation. Using cross-validation so scores reflect unseen data, not just the leaderboard.

The Titanic Survival Prediction project is one complete example.
